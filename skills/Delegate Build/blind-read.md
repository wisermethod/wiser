# Judging inputs

Apply at Brief step 3 before the judging read.

- Does the blind read have every input the work may rest on, in full, and every supplied summary checked against its source? No: supply missing inputs and correct summaries before the read. Yes: keep them.
- Is it a label-confirming read? Yes: also give the design's decision rule and every convention a fault depends on. No: add no label rules.
