## Authority

**Autonomous:** assembling boards from the parts library, rendering pages, running the named skills a stage hands to, writing records in the work folder, saving answers as they arrive, and the separately checked provisional writes of `design.md` under `skills/Build Brand/SKILL.md` Step 7, without B8.

## Execution Model

Each stage may be its own session, run by `skills/Build Brand/SKILL.md` Step 5 from this instance, which carries progress and the next stage between sessions. That skill governs wherever this master and it would differ; questions and their defaults come from `skills/Build Brand/questions.md`.

Has the person locked at F18? No: the round tasks stay open. In stage 4, set Progress's next action by the latest round answer, rather than to the next unticked task: no F14 yet, show the first page and collect F14; latest F14, show the blends, unless that answer asks for the first page again; latest F15, show the tuned board, unless it asks for the blends again; latest F16, ask what is next, and a round they name is that round; a board they would keep, ask F17, the dark check; F17 passed, ask F18; the lock when they say lock. A page whose Review task is open is reviewed before it is shown. Yes: close the round tasks from their evidence, recording a skipped blend or tuning round as skipped, not owed. Milestone 1 applies this rule on every resume.

Every page of boards passes the Creative Director's Review in a second context before the person sees it, and the brand in use receives its Audit in a second context (`experts/Creative Director/EXPERT.md`; `skills/Build Brand/SKILL.md` Step 5, stages 4 and 8). An unavailable Review follows stage 4's stated disclosure exception; an Audit decline is recorded under stage 8.

A stage whose skill or tool is unavailable names the stage, what it would have produced and what is owed, makes nothing in its place, and leaves dependent stages open (`skills/Build Brand/SKILL.md` Step 5).
