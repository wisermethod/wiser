# The sitting

Read only the section of `../questions.md` named at each step. Its words are the member's screens, not prompts to paraphrase. This path starts immediately, including inside onboarding, and ends complete at the lock. It writes no Playbook and asks no old F1 to F19 interview, dark check, mark decision or confirmation after the lock.

## Start and clock

Use `../records.md` for the answers record and `../SKILL.md` for ownership, kept assets, costs and voice. File board selections, assembled specs, returned forms, observations and source limits with the brand work in the owning root, normally `work/brand/`, following its filing declaration. Existing unrelated work there: resolve that collision before writing, never overwrite it. Keep an index to the last displayed page and answers record with that work, so a new session can resume it without a plan.

Is there a saved unfinished sitting? Yes: load its latest page, saved selections and answers, keep all shown histories, and pick up at those boards; with no page yet, resume the saved step, including a failed lettering setup, without re-asking answered screens. A saved brief or simple path resumes its own saved step rather than entering this sitting. No: show Questions Opening, then step 1 straight after it. Target the first boards at about minute 6 and the lock by minute 15. Allow about 15 seconds for opening, 20 for what to keep, one minute for words including drafting, 2:10 for taste including looks, one to three minutes for making and Review, 1:30 for boards, and about two minutes plus Review for each later round. Organisation adds about 45 seconds. These are targets, not a reason to skip checks: with three picks and twelve boards each round allow about 15 minutes, and first-time font setup adds a few minutes. Add no wait or approval beyond these screens.
