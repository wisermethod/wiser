# What the coach writes

Read at `paths/coach.md` step 7, for a personal root.

`voice.md` keeps the stub's standing sentence and preamble, carries the authority lines above the first section as `paths/from-writing.md` step 9 gives them, with `voice-authority-name:` the person's name as `memory/about.md`'s first heading spells it, `voice-authority-basis:` saying this is their own voice, confirmed by them on the piece they kept, or kept by them after two returns, whichever happened, and `voice-confirmation-date:` the day they said yes or kept it. Then these headings, in this order, every line citing the question numbers it rests on, or the samples, by their E numbers; a line the samples settled cites only the samples, never the question it stood in for:

- `## Portrait`: the portrait as the person confirmed it, in the third person, by their first name and then they, never a guessed pronoun, each sentence citing the answers it was read from and A1, where they confirmed it. A word of theirs on the closed list below is written as the move it stands for, read from their other answers.
- `## How They Write`: for each pair and each tap asked, the line `questions.md` gives for the person's answer, one for each kind picked at P6, copied word for word, with the pick's number; nothing for Neither, a skip, or a pair not asked. For each the samples settled, that option's line, word for word, citing the samples; then each move read from the samples, citing them, and where they said a sample had help, one line saying the samples included such writing and citing it. Where two of these lines meet in one sentence, the more cautious wins. Then each change sorted as how they say things and left in the voice, as one line naming the exact words or move, citing the change, such as `(A3)`, with `A3a` beside it where they moved it there; a change that says never goes under `## Prohibitions` instead, never under both. A change writes only the line it was about. Where a change contradicts a pick's line, or a pick or change contradicts a line read from the samples, the change's or the pick's line replaces it and cites both. Where nothing gives a line, the one line `- No moment settled yet; each piece decides (Research inference: W1 to W4).`
- `## Approved Lines`: one to three sentences from the piece as the person kept it, each followed by what makes it theirs and citing their yes (A2 or A4), or their keep (A6), and any change that shaped it; then the line `Study the pattern; never reuse these lines.` A sentence that carries something you made up is not one of them. What makes a line theirs never names one of the eight tells as their habit: a tell inside an approved line is theirs in that line only.
- `## Prohibitions`: W4 as concrete phrases or moves, in the words the person used, with the line they tapped at W4a as its example, and each change left in the voice that says never. No other pick writes a prohibition, and no prohibition names a word or phrase the person did not say or tap. Where nothing gives one, the line `- Nothing named yet (Research inference: W4).`
- `## Reading Ease`: R1's level and the line under it as `questions.md` gives them, or "Set by each piece's reader", written `(Firsthand: <their name>, R1)`; where the samples settled it, that level and line written `(Research inference: E1, E2)`, naming the samples; where R1 was skipped, "Set by each piece's reader (Research inference: R1)".
- `## Routing Table`: the stub's table, one row whose output type reads `Everything they write`, the register column naming the reading level and `the lines under How They Write`, the why column citing `(R1, W1 to W4)`, with the samples in place of R1 where they settled it.
- `## Requests Held to the Floor`: opens with the sentence "These are requests and habits held to the floor, not moves to copy; what each line says is written instead is the standard." Then each request below the floor, and each move of their samples that is one of the eight tells, citing the samples, with what is written instead; where none came, the one line `- No answer or sample asked for a habit below the floor (Research inference: W1 to W4).` A request recorded here is never also written as a line under another heading.

A heading answered in one sentence carries a register, since the gate reads a bare sentence as a deferral: `(Firsthand: <their name>, R1)` for what they said, `(Research inference: W4)` for what you read from their answers.
