# Hand-off checks

## Transfers

- Does a concern go to another build? Yes: put a pointer in the receiving Playbook or tell its session, and confirm it took it. No: go on.
- Is a step sent to another session? Yes: put its reasons in the Playbook the resume prompt opens. No: go on.

## Lesson intake

- Does the part already state the rule? Yes: do not write it again; a case it should have caught goes to Hand Off step 4's must-fire-every-time check. No: go on.
- Is the finding a defect in a part? Yes: fix it there or record it where the owning root keeps unbuilt work, never as a lesson. No: continue to Hand Off step 4's lesson-routing question.
