# Statement

A short public text in a named person's or organization's voice, issued to set a record: a holding line, a response, a confirmation, or a close. Read this file at Frame and hold it through Draft. A full news announcement is `press-release.md`. Spoken crisis remarks are `skills/Speech Writing/` with `occasions/crisis-remarks.md`.

Review expert: `experts/Ghost Writer/`. This root carries no crisis reviewer and no news-desk reviewer, so whether to speak at all in an unfolding incident, and what would make it worse, is not judged here; name that gap before the statement is issued.

## Shape

- **Length.** 80 to 200 words for a holding statement; up to 350 for a full statement. Over 400 is a release.
- **Structure.** Who is speaking, the fact, the action, what is not yet known if anything, a close that does not reopen the question.
- **Paragraphs.** Short. One idea each.
- **Stance.** First person plural or a named speaker. Attributable. No anonymous "sources said" in a statement the issuer is putting its name to.

## Format rules

- **Named speaker.** A person with a title, or the organization in its public voice. A statement with no speaker is a leak, not a statement.
- **Facts only.** What is known, what is being done, what comes next that can actually be promised. Speculation, motive, and blame stay out.
- **Holding vs full.** A holding statement buys time and names the next update. A full statement closes the question it was issued to close. Do not mix them.
- **No leftover marketing.** Superlatives, taglines, and "we remain committed to excellence" are not facts. Cut them.
- **Legal language.** If counsel supplied a constraint, it is a constraint, not a sentence to polish into something warmer. Flag a collision with voice rather than rewriting the constraint away.

## Failure modes

- **Filling silence with sympathy copy.** Empathy without a fact reads as evasion. Name the fact, then the care, not the reverse.
- **Answering a charge that was not made.** Opens a second story. Answer what was asked.
- **A promise no one can keep.** "We will ensure this never happens again" is not an action. Name the action underway.
- **Undated.** A statement without a date cannot be the record.

## Format checks

- The speaker is named.
- Every sentence is a fact, an action, or a named unknown.
- Holding or full is chosen, not blended.
- Ghost Writer review ran, and any crisis or news-desk gap was named.
