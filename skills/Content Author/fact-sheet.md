# Fact Sheet

A scannable brief of checkable claims about one subject: a company, a product, a filing, a campaign, a person. Read this file at Frame and hold it through Draft. A narrative argument is `op-ed.md` or `long-form.md`. A news announcement is `press-release.md`.

Review expert: `experts/Ghost Writer/`. This root carries no news-desk reviewer, so whether a reporter could lift the sheet as written is not judged here; name that gap.

## Shape

- **Length.** One or two pages. Over 800 words is a briefing paper, not a fact sheet.
- **Structure.** Title, one-sentence what this is, then labeled fields or short bullets grouped by topic. No narrative arc.
- **Stance.** Neutral, third person, present tense for what is true now. Dates on anything that can age.

## Format rules

- **One subject.** A fact sheet about three products is three sheets, or a comparison table with a reason.
- **Labeled claims.** Each line is a field a journalist can lift: founded, headquarters, what it does, scale, the number with its date, the named customer, the next milestone. A sentence of vision is not a field.
- **Sources on the sheet.** A figure, a ranking, or a person-fact carries its source and date on the same line, per `standards/conventions.md`. A sheet that sends the reader hunting has failed.
- **What it is not.** No lede, no quote block, no call to action. Those belong to the release or the pitch.
- **Currency.** Every number that can move carries an as-of date. A headcount with no date is a guess wearing a suit.

## Failure modes

- **Brochure.** Adjectives, benefits, and "leading provider" lines where a field should be. Test: can a reporter paste a line into a story without rewriting out the marketing? If not, it is not a fact.
- **Undated scale.** Users, revenue, countries, employees, with no when. Cut or date.
- **Buried news.** The one new fact sitting in paragraph four of a narrative. Put it in a labeled field at the top, or this is the wrong format.

## Format checks

- Every claim is a labeled field or a short parallel bullet, not a story.
- Every figure carries a source and an as-of date, or its evidence label.
- No quote, no lede, no call to action.
- Ghost Writer review ran, and any news-desk gap was named.
