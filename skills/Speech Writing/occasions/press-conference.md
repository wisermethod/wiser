# Press Conference

## Scope

A press conference is a live session with reporters, opened by a prepared statement and followed by questions. This guide is for the opening remarks. Q&A answers are a written pack under Content Author's `q-and-a.md`, or live answers the speaker owns; this skill does not prepare Q&A.

## Length target

Duration range: 3 to 8 minutes for the opening. At 130 to 150 words per minute, that is 390 to 1,200 words. Most land at 4 to 5 minutes, 520 to 750 words. Over 8 minutes starves the questions the room came for.

## Register

Plain, attributable, low-ceremony. The speaker is on the record. First-person plural or a named role. Warmth is allowed in one sentence of care; it is never the load-bearing move.

## Ritual beats

- **Who is speaking and why this room.** Title, not biography.
- **The news.** One sentence the cameras can lift.
- **The facts that make it true.** Dated, sourced, few.
- **What happens next.** An action, a timeline, a named next update.
- **Hand-off to questions.** Not a peroration.

## Occasion-specific conventions

- The audience is working. They will quote the first sentence and the last action. Both must stand alone.
- Numbers are spoken slowly and once. A second pass of the same figure sounds like spinning.
- Thank-yous, if required, sit after the news, never before it.
- Applause is not the architecture. A press conference that plays for applause has chosen the wrong room.
- Opening line patterns that hold: entering on the news ("This morning we filed..."), entering on the action ("We are recalling..."), entering on the known fact the room already has ("You have seen the video from last night. Here is what we know."). Entering on "thank you all for coming" spends the lift.

## Anti-patterns

- **The marketing open.** Vision, values, or "excited to share" before the news.
- **Reading the release.** If they can get it as a document, the remarks have to add a spoken throughline, not a second copy.
- **Previewing Q&A.** "I'm sure you'll ask about X" invites only X.
- **Closing on a slogan.** The last line is the action or the hand-off.

## Notes on arc adaptation

The universal arc compresses. Empathy anchor and curiosity gap collapse into the news sentence. The build is the facts. The turn is the action. The gift is the hand-off to questions. At 5 minutes the build is two to three minutes; everything else is short on purpose.
