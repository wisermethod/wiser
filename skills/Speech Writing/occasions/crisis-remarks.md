# Crisis Remarks

## Scope

Crisis remarks are spoken words in an unfolding incident: an accident, a breach, a misconduct allegation, a failure in public view. The occasion's function is to put facts, care, and action on the record without creating a second story. Written holding or full statements are Content Author's `statement.md`. This guide is for what is said aloud.

Judged by `experts/Ghost Writer/` for prose and voice. Whether to speak at all, in whose name, and what would make it worse is crisis judgment, which no primitive in this root supplies; name that gap before the remarks are delivered.

## Length target

Duration range: 1 to 3 minutes. At 130 to 150 words per minute, that is 130 to 450 words. Most holding remarks land under 2 minutes. Over 3 minutes is a speech the facts cannot yet support.

## Register

Quiet, specific, on the record. No performance. Empathy is a sentence about the people affected, not a paragraph about how the speaker feels. First person when the speaker is accountable; first person plural when the institution is.

## Ritual beats

- **The fact.** What is known, in one sentence.
- **The people affected.** Named as a group or, where it is right and sourced, as individuals. Not a pivot away from the fact.
- **The action underway.** What is being done now, not what is hoped.
- **What is not yet known.** Said as unknown, with when the next update comes.
- **Stop.** No vision close.

## Occasion-specific conventions

- Counsel's constraints are constraints. Do not warm them into a nicer sentence that changes their meaning.
- Blame, motive, and other parties' guilt stay out unless they are established facts the speaker is authorized to state.
- "We take this seriously" is not a beat. A named action is.
- Applause architecture: none. Silence after the last sentence is correct.
- Opening line patterns that hold: entering on the fact ("Two employees were injured at the plant this morning."), entering on the people ("Families of the passengers are being met as they arrive."), entering on the action ("We have taken the system offline."). Entering on values, journey, or "difficult time" postpones the only sentence the room needs.

## Anti-patterns

- **Filling the unknown.** Guessing a cause, a number, or a timeline to sound informed. The unknown is a beat; filling it is the next headline.
- **The apology that accepts a fact not yet known.** Apologize for what is established. Do not apologize for a narrative the investigation has not reached.
- **A rebound to brand.** "This is not who we are" after a harm. The remarks are about the harm.
- **A joke, even a small one.** Register-wrong.

## Notes on arc adaptation

The universal arc almost disappears. The fact is the empathy anchor and the curiosity gap together. The build is the action. There is no turn that reframes the incident into a lesson. The gift is the next update time. If the remarks need a lesson, it is too soon or it is the wrong occasion.
