Design Advisor's criteria are adapted, not copied, from two Apache 2.0 sources. **Both relationships were verified against the upstreams on 2026-09-02**, which replaces an earlier record that carried them forward from the attribution file of the primitive this one was converted from, read 2026-07-27, without checking them.

## Impeccable, by Paul Bakaus

**Copyright 2025 Paul Bakaus**, retained from the upstream source under Apache-2.0 section 4(c). Verified 2026-09-02 against that repository's own `LICENSE`, which carries exactly that line.

- Source: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- License: Apache 2.0
- What was drawn on: design principles, anti-pattern lists, and reference material from its skill and its reference files on typography, color and contrast, spatial design, motion, interaction, responsive behavior, and interface copy.
- How: read, distilled, and rewritten twice, once for the system this primitive was converted from and again against this root's standards. No passage is verbatim, and the raw source is not redistributed here.
- What the verification found: the upstream's reference files are named `typeset`, `colorize`, `layout`, `animate`, `clarify`, `adapt` and `bolder`, which is the list above; and the operation vocabulary in `experts/Creative Director/` carries five of that project's command names, with `bolder` and `quieter` as a matched pair. The debt is real and is larger than an earlier reading of it assumed.

## frontend-design, by Anthropic

**Copyright is held by Anthropic.** Verified 2026-09-02: the skill is published under Apache 2.0 at the source below, and it ships no `NOTICE` file, so Apache-2.0 section 4(d) does not attach to it.

- Source: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design
- License: Apache 2.0
- What was drawn on: grounding a design in its own subject's world, and spending boldness on a single signature element. Neither is verbatim. The three whole-layout clusters drawn from the same source now live in the Prohibited Defaults Taxonomy in `experts/Creative Director/EXPERT.md`, whose NOTICE carries their attribution.

**A claim this file used to make and no longer does.** An earlier version said Impeccable builds on the Anthropic skill, and so was the foundation under the material above. **Neither party says that.** Impeccable's own `NOTICE.md` credits one upstream, `ehmo/platform-design-skills` under MIT, and never mentions Anthropic; the Anthropic skill never mentions Impeccable. The claim was inherited unverified and is withdrawn rather than restated. The two sources are recorded here independently, which is all the evidence supports.

Impeccable ships a `NOTICE.md`, so Apache-2.0 section 4(d) applies to it. **It was read on 2026-09-02.** Its only attribution covers `skill/reference/ios.md` and `skill/reference/android.md`, distilled from `ehmo/platform-design-skills` under MIT. **Nothing here draws on those two files**, so that notice does not pertain to any part of this derivative work and is excluded by 4(d)'s own exclusion clause.
